References and credit

Every paper below was looked up in a live literature index and has a working DOI. None of it was written from memory.

Credit for the data

All the credit for designing this study, finding participants and running the scanner belongs to Kapil, Kim, McAvoy and Philip at Washington University in St. Louis, funded by NINDS grant R01 NS114046. They released everything under CC0, which is what made a project like this possible at all.

This site is a student reanalysis. It is not connected to that group, they have not reviewed it, and any mistakes in it are mine. Their own write-up is the preprint linked below and it should be read as the authoritative account.

The list

  • Precision drawing during task fMRI in healthy adults and individuals with peripheral nerve injury
    Kapil N, Kim T, McAvoy MP, Philip BA, 2026. OpenNeuro (dataset). doi:10.18112/openneuro.ds008162.v1.0.3
    The scans used here. Released under CC0.
  • Study accompanying the drawing fMRI dataset
    Philip BA and colleagues, 2025. bioRxiv preprint. doi:10.1101/2025.11.18.689091
    Listed by the dataset authors as the related publication.
  • Reassessing cortical reorganization in the primary sensorimotor cortex following arm amputation
    Makin TR, Scholz J, Slater D, Johansen-Berg H, Tracey I, 2015. Brain 138(8):2140. doi:10.1093/brain/awv161
    Found that the link between cortical remapping and phantom pain did not hold up once other factors were measured. A direct warning that remapping results need hard controls.
  • Reproducible brain-wide association studies require thousands of individuals
    Marek S, Tervo-Clemmens B, Calabro FJ, and 42 others, 2022. Nature. doi:10.1038/s41586-022-04492-9
    Brain to behaviour effects are smaller than people assumed, and small samples give inflated results that do not replicate. The main reason this project reports permutation tests and does not lead with accuracy.
  • Small sample sizes reduce the replicability of task-based fMRI studies
    Turner BO, Paul EJ, Miller MB, Barbey AK, 2018. Communications Biology. doi:10.1038/s42003-018-0073-z
    Same problem, measured for task fMRI rather than resting state.
  • An evaluation of the efficacy, reliability, and sensitivity of motion correction strategies for resting-state functional MRI
    Parkes L, Fulcher B, Yücel M, Fornito A, 2018. NeuroImage. doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2017.12.073
    Compared 19 denoising pipelines. Showed that group differences in connectivity depend heavily on how motion is handled, which is why the motion-only model is reported here.
  • Cluster failure: why fMRI inferences for spatial extent have inflated false-positive rates
    Eklund A, Nichols TE, Knutsson H, 2016. PNAS. doi:10.1073/pnas.1602413113
    Showed permutation tests behave correctly where parametric ones did not.
  • BrainGB: a benchmark for brain network analysis with graph neural networks
    Cui H, Dai W, Zhu Y, and 7 others, 2022. IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging. doi:10.1109/tmi.2022.3218745
    Benchmark for graph networks on brain data, and the source of the model design choices used here.
  • Local-global parcellation of the human cerebral cortex from intrinsic functional connectivity MRI
    Schaefer A, Kong R, Gordon EM, and 5 others, 2018. Cerebral Cortex 28(9):3095. doi:10.1093/cercor/bhx179
    The 200 cortical regions used as graph nodes.
  • Automated anatomical labeling of activations in SPM using a macroscopic anatomical parcellation of the MNI MRI single-subject brain
    Tzourio-Mazoyer N, Landeau B, Papathanassiou D, and 5 others, 2002. NeuroImage. doi:10.1006/nimg.2001.0978
    Source of the 26 cerebellar regions added to the graph.
  • Reorganization of human cerebral cortex: the range of changes following use and injury
    Elbert T, Rockstroh B, 2004. The Neuroscientist. doi:10.1177/1073858403262111
    Review of how use and injury change cortical maps.
  • Greater cortical activation and motor recovery following mirror therapy immediately after peripheral nerve repair of the forearm
    Chen YH, Siow TY, Wang JY, and 2 others, 2021. Neuroscience. doi:10.1016/j.neuroscience.2021.11.048
    One of the few fMRI studies of peripheral nerve injury in the hand and forearm.
  • Disability, atrophy and cortical reorganization following spinal cord injury
    Freund P, Weiskopf N, Ward NS, and 6 others, 2011. Brain 134(6):1610. doi:10.1093/brain/awr093
    Linked the amount of remapping to how disabled someone was. The model for the severity analysis used here.

Software

ANTs through antspyx for lining up brains, taking out head movement and splitting tissue. nibabel and nilearn for reading files, cleaning signals and working out connectivity. scikit-learn for the ordinary models. PyTorch and PyTorch Geometric for the graph networks. Captum for working out which regions mattered. TemplateFlow for the reference brain. All free and open source.
